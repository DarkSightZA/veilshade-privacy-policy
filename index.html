Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Introduction

VeilShade is an Android privacy application designed to help protect on-screen conversations from being easily viewed by people nearby.

This Privacy Policy explains what information VeilShade processes, how that information is used, and how third-party services integrated into the application may process information.

2. Information VeilShade Does Not Collect

VeilShade is designed so that the application does not collect or transmit the contents of your private conversations.

  • We do not collect your chat messages.
  • We do not collect the text you type into other applications.
  • We do not collect your contacts.
  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not require your name, phone number, or email address to use the app.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3. Android Permissions and System Information

Display over other apps

VeilShade requires Android's overlay permission so it can display the privacy Veil and its floating control above other applications.

Keyboard and window awareness

VeilShade uses Android window and keyboard information to determine when the keyboard is visible and where the Veil should be positioned. This allows the application to keep the keyboard visible and usable while protecting the conversation area above it.

This system-level processing is used to provide the app's privacy overlay functionality. VeilShade does not use this functionality to read, collect, or transmit the contents of your conversations.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

VeilShade may store application settings and entitlement information locally on your device so that your preferences and access state can persist between sessions.

Examples may include selected appearance settings, Veil preferences, feature settings, and temporary Pro access state.

This locally stored information is used to operate the application and is not intended to contain the contents of your private chats.

5. Google Play Billing

VeilShade offers an optional permanent Pro upgrade through Google Play Billing.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. VeilShade does not directly receive or store your payment card number, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

The application may receive purchase and entitlement information from Google Play, such as whether the Pro purchase has been completed and acknowledged. This information is used to provide the features you purchased and to restore your entitlement when appropriate.

Google's handling of payment and account information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

6. Rewarded Advertisements

VeilShade may display rewarded advertisements using Google Mobile Ads / AdMob.

When you choose to watch a rewarded advertisement, Google and its advertising partners may process information according to their own privacy policies. This may include device, advertising, diagnostic, and usage information as permitted by their services and your applicable settings.

VeilShade uses rewarded advertisements to provide temporary Pro access after a qualifying rewarded-ad event.

VeilShade does not control the information independently collected or processed by Google Mobile Ads. For more information, please review Google's applicable privacy documentation.

7. Third-Party Services

VeilShade may use third-party services necessary for specific application functionality, including:

  • Google Play Services and Google Play Billing for in-app purchases.
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob for rewarded advertisements.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies. Their processing is separate from VeilShade's handling of your application settings and overlay functionality.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

VeilShade does not maintain a VeilShade account containing your personal information.

Application settings and locally stored application data remain on your device until they are cleared, removed by the Android system, or the application is uninstalled, subject to Android's normal behavior and any applicable backup settings.

Information handled by third-party services such as Google Play or AdMob is subject to the respective provider's retention and deletion practices.

9. Children's Privacy

VeilShade is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because VeilShade does not require an account and is not designed to collect private conversation content, it does not intentionally create user profiles containing children's personal information.

Third-party services integrated into the application may have their own age requirements and privacy practices.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by VeilShade. The application is designed to process its core privacy overlay functionality on the device rather than transmitting private conversation content to our servers.

However, no software or electronic transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when VeilShade's functionality, services, or legal requirements change.
